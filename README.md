# The Cup

A short poem about permission — the power to ask and to be given.

> He poured my tea each morning,
> two sugars, no asking.
>
> Now his hands tremble.
> The pot is too heavy now.
> Let me, I say,
> and steady the pot —
> one slow amber stream —
> and set the cup in his hands,
> the pour still his to offer.

For years he poured for me without being asked; now I steady the pot for him — but the pour, the offering itself, is still his. The gift is not the tea; it is the hand that keeps offering.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the poem above, exactly as written, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falling back to `/output/entry.txt`), and validates that it is 1–280 characters.
